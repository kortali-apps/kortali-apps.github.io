Security measures

  • Hosting: Atlassian Forge only ("Runs on Atlassian"). No Provider servers and no third-party services. Atlassian's platform encrypts data in transit and at rest.
  • No data egress: the app calls only Atlassian APIs. No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Access control: Jira data is read with the permissions of the person using the app. Creating, changing and deleting requirement sets, and deleting baselines, need Jira project administrator rights, checked on the server.
  • Data minimisation: only the issue fields the Customer selects are stored, and personal data is limited as described above. Stored account IDs are reported to Atlassian every week and closed accounts are erased.
  • No vendor access: Provider has no access to the data the app stores, and the app writes no personal data to its logs.
  • Integrity: each baseline carries a chained SHA-256 fingerprint that the app can verify.
  • Secure development: server-side validation of every input, safe CSV and HTML exports, read-only Jira scopes, automated tests and dependency scanning before each release.
  • Accounts: Atlassian's mandatory two-step verification protects the account that publishes the app.
  • Incidents: Provider notifies the Customer of a Security Incident without undue delay and within 48 hours, as Section 5.2 of the Bonterms DPA requires, and notifies Atlassian as the Marketplace Partner Agreement requires.
