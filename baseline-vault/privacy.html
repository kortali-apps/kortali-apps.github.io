Privacy policy – Baseline Vault for Jira

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Vendor: Kortali Apps (Abdelfattah Kortali, Algeria). Contact: hello@provecore.com.

What the app does with data

Baseline Vault runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian"). It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

Data the app processes

  • Jira issue data in the requirement sets you define: key, summary, description, status, priority, issue type, links to other issues (key, type, status, summary) and any extra fields an administrator selects. The app reads it with the permissions of the person using the app.
  • Requirement set settings created by project administrators (name, JQL, issue and link types, field list).
  • Baseline records: the frozen issue data above, plus the baseline's name, note, creation time, and the Atlassian account ID of the person who created it.

Personal data

The only personal data the app stores is the Atlassian account ID of the person who created a baseline.

  • Names, email addresses and avatars are never stored. The creator's name is looked up from Jira each time it is shown.
  • Fields that hold people (assignee, reporter, user pickers) are never recorded in baselines. @mentions in descriptions are recorded as "@user".
  • Once a week the app reports the stored account IDs to Atlassian's personal data reporting API, as Atlassian requires of apps. When Atlassian reports an account as closed, the app erases that account ID from every baseline, which then shows "Former user". The frozen requirement content and its fingerprint are not affected.

Where it is stored

Only in Atlassian's Forge storage for your Atlassian site. Data residency follows your site's settings as provided by Atlassian. We (the vendor) cannot access your stored data.

What the app does not do

No cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no sale or sharing of data, no transfer outside Atlassian.

Retention and deletion

Requirement sets and baselines remain until a project administrator deletes them in the app. If you uninstall the app, Atlassian deletes the app's stored data for your site according to the Forge platform's data retention rules.

Your rights

Your organisation controls the data. For any request about personal data processed through the app, contact your Atlassian site administrator, or us at hello@provecore.com; we will help where we can, although we hold no copy.
