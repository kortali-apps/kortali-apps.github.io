Support

Support for apps by Kortali Apps, including Baseline Vault for Jira.

Contact: hello@provecore.com · Hours: Sunday–Thursday, 08:00–17:00 Central European Time (UTC+1); we answer in English or French.

Response targets:

  • Critical (app unusable for the whole site, data integrity question): first answer within 24 hours, every day.
  • Other questions and bugs: within 2 business days.
  • Feature requests: acknowledged within 5 business days.

Please include: your site URL, the project key, what you did, what you expected, and a screenshot if possible. Never send passwords or API tokens.

Security issues: hello@provecore.com.
